FAQ

Common questions

What can ECSPY analyze?
ECSPY analyzes any website for traffic, revenue estimates, keywords and rankings. For Shopify and WooCommerce it also detects apps/plugins, themes, best-selling products, active ads, supplier sources and Whois data.
How does the credit system work?
Credits power premium features. Store spy costs 2 credits, AI queries cost 1 credit, supplier lookups cost 3 credits, and bulk analysis costs 5 credits per store. Credits reset monthly and unused credits roll over for 90 days.
Is the AI assistant really free?
Yes — the AI uses 1 credit per query. Free plan users get 5 AI queries per day. Pro and Business users get unlimited AI access. No separate API key or external service is required — it's built directly into ECSPY.
How accurate is revenue estimation?
Revenue estimates combine traffic data, niche conversion benchmarks, product pricing and order frequency signals. They're directionally accurate and best used to compare stores against each other rather than as exact figures.
Can I export data to CSV?
Yes — Pro and Business users can export any analyzed data to CSV with a single click. The Chrome extension also supports on-page CSV export for Shopify and WooCommerce product catalogs.
